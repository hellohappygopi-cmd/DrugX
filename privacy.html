Privacy Policy — DrugX

Last updated: October 2026

DrugX ("the app") is made by the DrugX team. It helps you remember your medicines and keep a record of your sugar and blood pressure readings.

The short version

  • Everything stays on your phone. There is no account, no sign-up and no server.
  • Nothing is sent anywhere. The app never uses the internet (the Android version does not even have permission to). We cannot see your information.
  • No automatic copies. The Android app is left out of the phone's automatic Google backup and of phone-to-phone transfer; a backup file is made only when you ask.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and no crash reporting of its own. If you share usage and diagnostics with Google, Google may give us basic crash details from the app (for example the phone model and where the app stopped), never your medicines or readings.
  • You decide what to share, and with whom.

What the app keeps, and where

The app keeps the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • medicines you add (name, strength, how to take it, pill color, photo, pill count, expiry date);
  • your schedule and your answers to reminders (taken, skipped, missed, remind me later);
  • sugar and blood pressure readings you enter;
  • your settings, including an optional helper's name and phone number.

We (the developer) never receive any of this. We do not collect, store, sell or share personal or health information, and no third party receives it through the app.

On Android the app is left out of the phone's automatic Google backup and of phone-to-phone transfer, so none of this is copied to your Google account or to a new phone. To move to a new phone, make a backup file in the app (Settings › Family, doctor and backup › Backup) and keep it where you choose.

The app does not use the internet, your location, contacts, microphone or advertising ID. On Android it does not even ask for internet access; iPhone apps cannot turn it off, but this app never connects.

Camera and reading labels

When you add a medicine by photo, the words on the box are read on your phone (Android: Google ML Kit text recognition running on the device; iPhone: Apple's built-in text recognition). The result only fills in suggestions — name, strength, expiry date — that you check before anything is saved. The picture is kept with that medicine on your phone.

On your phone's screens

  • Home screen widgets (Android) show your next medicine and its time, or today's doses. Anyone who can see your home screen can see them; you choose whether to add a widget.
  • Reminders show the medicine's name and how many pills to take, and when, and notes about running low or a medicine expiring show its name, also on the lock screen.
  • Read aloud uses your phone's own speech engine; DrugX itself sends nothing.

Sharing is always your choice

The app can make a PDF report for your doctor, a backup file, or a message for your helper (for example a missed-dose note). These are created only when you tap a share button, and your phone's own share screen lets you choose where they go (for example email, WhatsApp or Google Drive). Once shared, that copy is handled by the app or person you chose.

Contact support (Settings › About) opens your own email app with a message to us that contains only the app version and your phone's model and system version, no health information. Nothing is sent unless you press send. If you write to us, we use your email only to answer you.

Deleting your data

  • In the app: Settings › About › Privacy and your data › Delete all my data removes all medicines, records, readings, photos, settings and reminders from your phone.
  • Uninstalling the app also deletes everything it stored. No copy is kept in your Google account, because the app is left out of the phone's automatic backup.
  • Backup files or reports you saved elsewhere (Drive, WhatsApp, email) are not touched by the app; delete them there.

Children

The app is meant for adults and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Not a medical device

DrugX is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It is a reminder and record-keeping tool and gives no medical advice, dosing suggestions or drug-interaction checks. Always follow your doctor's advice. See also the Terms of use.
