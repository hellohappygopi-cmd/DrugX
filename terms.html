Terms of Use — DrugX

Last updated: October 2026

These terms apply when you use DrugX ("the app"), made by the DrugX team.

What DrugX does

DrugX reminds you when to take your medicines and keeps your sugar and blood pressure readings. It is a tool to help you remember. It is not a medical device.

Not medical advice

DrugX does not diagnose, give medical advice or suggest doses, and it does not check how medicines work together. Always follow your doctor or pharmacist. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Reminders depend on your phone

Reminders need notifications to be allowed, and battery saving or other phone settings can delay or stop them. Check that reminders ring on your phone, and keep your own routine as well. We cannot promise that every reminder arrives.

Your data

Everything you enter stays on your phone, as described in the Privacy Policy. Keeping a backup is up to you; DrugX can make one when you ask.

Using DrugX

You may use DrugX for yourself, or to help someone you care for with their agreement. Please check the details you enter, including anything read from a photo.

No guarantee

DrugX is provided as it is. As far as the law allows, its makers are not responsible for harm from a missed, late or wrong reminder, reading or report.
